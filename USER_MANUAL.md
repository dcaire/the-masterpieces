# The Masterpieces — User Manual

*Texas Master Chorale · Masterpieces — booking, ensemble & repertoire manager*

**App URL:** https://the-masterpieces-app.netlify.app

This guide covers everything the app does, organized the way you actually work. If you only read one section, read **Bookings** — it's the heart of the app.

---

## 1. The big picture

The app has **two separate experiences**:

| Who | What they open | What they see |
|---|---|---|
| **Beth / managers** | the main app URL | Everything — dashboard, bookings, events, music, prospects, archive |
| **Singers** | their *personal* portal link | Only their own schedule, RSVP, sheet music, and "can't sing" calendar |

A singer never sees your bookings, revenue, or other admin — just their own stuff.

**Tip:** the app is a website, so it works on a laptop, iPad, or phone, and there's nothing to install. If something looks stale after an update, do a **hard refresh** (Mac: ⌘+Shift+R, Windows: Ctrl+Shift+R).

## 3. Dashboard

Your home screen and daily snapshot.

- **Email status chip** (top right): 🟢 *Email connected* means Send Now is live; 🟠 *not configured* means it still needs setup (see §11).
- **Stat cards:** The Ensemble (voice count), Cloud Library (songs), Follow-ups Due, and **Revenue** (this year + all-time). Click any card to jump to that area.
- **Calendar:** a month view with **🟢 green dots = events**, **🟡 amber = bookings**, **🔴 red = a singer is unavailable** that day. Tap a day to see what's on; tap an item to open it. Use **‹ ›** to change months, **Today** to snap back. Below the calendar, **"Who's away · upcoming"** lists each singer's blackout periods so you can plan around them.
- **Next Performance** card: your soonest upcoming event and who's confirmed.
- **The Four Voices:** a quick look at your S/A/T/B sections.

## 4. Ensemble — your singers

Everyone who sings (or directs).

- **Director/Manager** (Beth) shows at the top as a banner — she's *not* in the singer lists and can't be picked for events.
- **Core members** and **Guest singers** are listed separately.
- **Add Singer:** top-right button → name, voice part, role (core or guest).
- **Tap a singer** to open their card. From there you can:
  - **Copy link / Email it** — sends them their **private Singer Portal** link (§12).
  - **Edit Details**, **Mark Inactive / Reactivate**, **→ Make Guest / Make Core**, or **Delete**.

**Guests:** subs you bring in for a single event. You can add a guest on the fly from a booking's lineup too (see §6).

## 5. Bookings — the heart of the app

A **booking** is an inquiry → a potential performance. The app walks you from "someone asked" all the way to "it's on the calendar."

### 5a. Creating a booking — the wizard
Tap **New Inquiry**. A guided, 4-step wizard appears:
1. **Who's asking?** — contact name + organization (required), phone, email.
2. **What's the event?** — date + occasion.
3. **What will you send?** — pick a **format**. This is the important one: each format sets **how many singers you need**:
   - Quartet = 4 · Trio = 3 · Duo = 2 · Soloist = 1 · Quintet = 5 · Sextet = 6 · **DJ = 0 (no singers)**
   - Plus the expected fee.
4. **Anything else?** — notes, then a **review** of everything → **Create Booking**.

### 5b. The booking list
Each card shows the status and a **lineup chip** at a glance:
- `Quartet · 2/4` (amber — still filling) · `Trio ✓` (green — covered) · `lineup TBD` (grey — not started) · `DJ` (teal).
Overdue follow-ups get a red left edge and an **"overdue"** banner at the top. The list sorts by soonest event.

### 5c. Inside a booking — the "Next step" coach
Open any booking and a black **"Next step"** card tells you exactly what to do next and gives you the button to do it. The flow is:

**Details → Lineup → Availability → Confirm → Event.**

**Building the lineup & checking availability:**
- The **Singer Availability** panel lists your singers. **Tap a singer's ＋** to add them to this event's lineup. Mark each one **yes / maybe / no** as they reply.
- Anyone who blocked that date in their portal is flagged **"✕ Away this date"** automatically — so you don't have to email to ask.
- **+ Guest** brings in a sub on the spot.
- **Email Availability** sends a branded "are you in?" email to the singers you picked (it pulls their emails automatically).
- The panel shows **"X / N confirmed."** When you have enough yeses, it turns green: *"Lineup covered."*

**If you can't fill the lineup**, you have three outs:
- **+ Invite a guest singer**, or
- **↓ Downsize** to a smaller format (Quartet→Trio→Duo…), or
- **Make it a DJ engagement** (0 singers).

**Confirm & convert:**
- **Confirm with client** marks it confirmed (you can confirm even if not fully covered — it'll say "anyway").
- **Add to Events** turns the confirmed booking into a real event, carrying the format, lineup, and availability over.

**Other actions:** Compose Email, Edit, Log Follow-up (auto-sets the next nudge), Mark Contacted, Mark Lost, **Delete**.

## 6. Events — upcoming performances

Confirmed/upcoming performances live here (past ones move to **Archive**, §10). Opening an event lets you:

- **Manage the lineup** (same Singer Availability panel as bookings).
- **Pick songs** — the **Program** picker pulls from your Music library. Each chosen song shows an **iPad** or **Cloud** tag (whether it's loaded on the singers' iPads).
- **Send Proposal** — emails the program (the song list) to the client.
- See the event's **contact and notes**, **Edit Event**, or **Delete**.
- The **"Next step"** coach here nudges: Lineup → Program → Proposal.

---

## 7. Music — your repertoire library

Two things in one: your **song library** and a **one-tap iPad loader**.

- **Upload Arrangement:** title, arranger, category, pages, file size, a **Download link** (a shareable PDF/Drive URL singers can download), and a destination.
- Each piece is **🟢 On iPads** or **⚪ Cloud only** — **tap to flip** it, pushing/pulling that chart to every singer's iPad at once.
- The teal panel up top shows how many pieces are on the iPads and storage used.
- **Search** by title/arranger and **filter** by category.
- The **Download link** is what powers the singers' download buttons in their portal — paste a shareable link per song and it appears for them.

## 8. Prospects — leads & past clients (your CRM)

Everyone worth reaching out to: cold leads *and* every past client.

- **Past clients** (pulled from your event history) show a **★ client** badge and **Booked** status, with their booking history in the notes.
- **Stat tiles** by type (Service & Social Club, Church/Faith, Senior Living, Club/Venue) — tap to filter.
- **Filters:** status chips + "Only show" toggles (has email / contact / phone) + search.
- **Tap a prospect** to see contact info, notes, and actions: **Compose Email**, **Mark Contacted/Interested**, **Convert to Booking**, **Edit**, **Pass**.

### 8a. The Stay-in-touch drip
Keep past clients warm with periodic "we're available" reminders.
- On a prospect, **Start drip** schedules a reminder (default every 90 days).
- The Prospects tab shows a **"reminders due"** banner; cards show a **drip / reminder-due** pill.
- **Send reminder** opens a pre-written **"Stay in Touch"** email, logs it, and schedules the next one.
- *(Currently you click to send each reminder — it's guided, not fully automatic. Ask if you'd like it automated.)*

## 10. Archive — past performances

Every performance that's already happened, kept in full for your records and re-engagement.

- **Search** past events by name, contact, or notes; sorted most-recent-first.

## 11. Email — sending from the app

The app sends **fully branded** emails (logo, your colors) from **info@themasterpieces.org** (replies come to your Gmail).

- **Compose Email** (from a booking, prospect, event, or archived client) opens the composer.
- **Templates:** Cold Intro, Initial Outreach, Gentle Follow-up, Booking Confirmation, Thank You, **Stay in Touch** (drip), plus the **Availability Request** (to singers) and **Program Proposal** (to clients).
- **Buttons:**
  - **Send Now** — sends the branded email instantly (this is the main one).
  - **Open in Mail App** — opens your mail program with a plain-text version.
  - **Copy Formatted** — copies the branded version to paste into Gmail.
- The **Recipients** line fills automatically (the lead/singer emails you've picked).

**Status:** the chip on the Dashboard and in the composer shows whether email is **connected**.

**One-time setup (admin):** email works through a service called **Resend** with a verified domain. If you ever see "not configured," the Resend API key needs to be set in Supabase. (See §14.)

## 12. The Singer Portal — what your singers see

Each singer opens their **private link** (from Ensemble → Copy/Email link). No password. They see:

- **Their upcoming events** — date, venue.
- **RSVP** — Yes / Maybe / No, which writes straight back to your availability tracking (so singers can self-confirm).
- **"Singing with you"** — the rest of that event's lineup.
- **Music** — the program, with **Download** buttons for each piece (when you've added a download link).
- **"When you can't sing"** calendar — they tap days they're unavailable. **You see those instantly** (red flags on bookings/events and the dashboard) — no need to email and ask.
- **Director contact** — a one-tap email/call button to reach Beth.

## 13. Tips, FAQ & troubleshooting

- **Something looks old / didn't update?** Hard refresh (⌘/Ctrl + Shift + R).
- **A screen went blank?** The app now shows a "Something went wrong — Reload" message with the error; tap Reload, and if it persists, send Beth/the developer that message.
- **Email says "not configured"?** The Resend key isn't set (see §14).
- **Singer download says "Not posted yet"?** That song has no download link — add one in Music → edit the arrangement.
- **A singer shouldn't be in the list?** Mark them inactive, or set them as Director (directors are excluded from singer lists).
- **Deleting** anything asks you to confirm, and removes related availability records — it can't be undone.

## 14. Behind the scenes (for whoever set it up)

- **Hosting/data:** the app is a static site on **Netlify**; data lives in **Supabase** (Postgres). Every push to the repo auto-deploys.
- **Email:** a Supabase Edge Function (`send-email`) sends via **Resend**. The API key lives in Supabase (`RESEND_API_KEY` secret or the `app_config` table). The sending domain **themasterpieces.org** is verified in Resend; the "from" is **info@themasterpieces.org**, reply-to the group Gmail.
- **Singer portals:** a second Edge Function (`member-portal`) serves each singer's data by their private token — it never exposes manager data.
- **Imported data:** the event history (revenue spreadsheet) and the past-client prospects were loaded from Beth's files.
